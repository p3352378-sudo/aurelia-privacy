Aurelia – Delete your data

Effective: 21 October 2026

Delete everything from inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open Aurelia and tap your profile letter (top corner of Home).
  2. Scroll to the bottom of Profile and tap Delete my data and sign out.
  3. Confirm. Your tasks, routines, notes, drawings, chat history and settings are erased, your reminders are cancelled, and your name and email are removed from the phone.

If you can no longer open the app

  • Clear the app's storage: Android Settings > Apps > Aurelia > Storage > Clear storage. Or uninstall the app.
  • Optionally remove Aurelia's access to your Google account: Google Account > Security > Sign in with Google.

What we hold

Aurelia has no server of its own that stores your data. Everything lives on your phone, so deleting it there deletes it completely. We hold no copy elsewhere.

Your subscription

Deleting data does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play: Play Store > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > Aurelia.
