Aurelia

Personal assistance for your day

Aurelia is an Android app that helps you balance work and health. Talk to it, and it plans your day, keeps your notes and reminds you of what matters.

What Aurelia does

  • Schedule tasks by voice for today, the week, the month or the year, with repeating routines
  • Reminders on time, even after your phone restarts
  • Reminders for water, workouts and medicines, if you want them
  • Notes that you can type or draw
  • A talk button on your home screen, notification shade and lock screen
  • A gentle morning nudge to plan your day when nothing is scheduled

Your privacy

You sign in with Google, and the app only receives your name and email. Your tasks, notes and chat history stay on your phone. Nothing you create is sent to our servers, because the app has none.

Subscription

Aurelia has a free trial for new subscribers, then a low monthly subscription handled by Google Play. You can cancel any time in Google Play.
