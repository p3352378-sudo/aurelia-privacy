Aurelia – Privacy Policy

Effective: 21 October 2026

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Aurelia Daily Assistant app ("Aurelia", "the app", "we") handles your information. It applies to the Android app only, wherever you use it. The app is provided by an individual developer; you can contact us at the address in section 15.

By tapping "Continue with Google" and signing in you confirm that you have read this policy and agree to it. If you do not agree, please do not sign in and uninstall the app.

2. Information we handle

  • Google account details: your name and email address, received from Google when you sign in. We do not receive your Google password, profile photo, contacts or any other Google data.
  • What you create in the app: tasks, reminders, repeating routines, notes and drawings, water, workout and medicine reminders, your conversation history with the assistant, and your settings (assistant name, voice, theme and so on).
  • Your voice: when you tap the microphone (in the app, the home-screen widget, the quick-settings tile or the notification button) the app listens to understand your command. The app does not record, save or upload your voice. Speech is converted to text by the speech-recognition service built into your phone, which depending on your phone and its settings may be provided by Google and may process audio online under Google's own policies.
  • Subscription status: Google Play tells the app whether your subscription is active, including whether you are in the free-trial period. The free trial and all payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card, UPI or bank details.
  • Advertising information: the app shows one small advertisement in a dedicated space on the Home screen (see section 8). The company that supplies that advertisement may collect certain device information, described in section 8.
  • Basic technical information kept on your phone: when your subscription was last confirmed with Google Play, and whether you are online.

3. How we use it

  • To create your profile, greet you by name and show your initial in the app.
  • To store, show and remind you about your tasks, notes and routines, and to understand your spoken or typed commands.
  • To check with Google Play that your subscription or free trial is active, so that the app works for you and only for active subscribers.
  • To keep the app working, for example reminders after you restart your phone.

We do not sell your information. We do not use your name, email, tasks, notes, drawings or voice to choose or target advertisements, and we do not share them with advertisers.

4. Where your information is stored

Your tasks, notes, drawings, reminders, chat history and settings are stored only on your phone. The app has no server of its own that receives them, so we cannot read them, and the advertising service never receives them. Your name and email are also kept on your phone. If you turn on Android's backup for your phone, Android may copy the app's data to your own Google account as part of the phone's backup; that is controlled by you in your phone's settings.

5. Who else may receive information

We do not share your information with anyone, except these services that the app relies on and that have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Sign-In (to log you in).
  • Google Play and Google Play Billing (to run the subscription).
  • The third-party advertising service that supplies the advertisement on the Home screen (see section 8).
  • The speech-recognition and text-to-speech services on your phone, which may be provided by Google.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

6. Health-related reminders

Water, workout and medicine reminders are simple reminders that you set. They are stored only on your phone and are not shared. Aurelia is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Always follow your doctor's instructions.

7. Permissions the app asks for

  • Microphone: to hear your voice commands when you ask it to listen.
  • Notifications: to show reminders and the Talk button.
  • Exact alarms and starting at boot: so reminders ring on time, even after the phone restarts.
  • Foreground service (microphone): to listen while you use the Talk button without opening the app.
  • Internet and network state: to sign in, to check your subscription, to show the advertisement on the Home screen, and to know whether you are online.
  • Advertising ID: the advertising service on the Home screen may use your phone's advertising ID (see section 8).

You can switch any permission off in your phone's settings; the features that need it will then stop working.

8. Advertisements

The app has one small, dedicated space for an advertisement, and it is on the Home screen only. There are no advertisements anywhere else in the app: none on the Schedule, Health, Notes, Settings or sign-in screens, none while you are talking to the assistant, and no pop-ups, full-screen or sound-playing advertisements. The space is separate from the rest of the screen, so it does not cover or move your schedule, your reminders or the Chat and microphone buttons.

The advertisement is supplied by a third-party advertising service (for example, Google AdMob). To show and measure advertisements, that service may collect and use information from your phone such as your advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device model and operating-system version, app version, and whether an advertisement was shown or tapped. It may use this to choose advertisements, to limit how often you see one, and to prevent fraud, under its own privacy policy.

We do not give the advertising service your name, email, tasks, notes, drawings, chat history or voice, and nothing you create in the app is used to select advertisements.

You can limit this on your phone: in Android Settings, open Google (or Privacy) > Ads, then turn off ads personalisation or delete your advertising ID. You will still see an advertisement, but it will be less relevant to you. Tapping an advertisement opens the advertiser's own page or app, which is not covered by this policy.

If we change the advertising service or how advertisements work, we will update this policy.

9. How long we keep information, and deleting it

Your information stays on your phone until you remove it. You can delete any task, routine or note inside the app.

To erase everything in one step, open Profile in the app and tap "Delete my data and sign out". This permanently deletes your tasks, routines, notes, drawings, chat history and settings, cancels your reminders, and removes your name and email from the phone. We hold no copy anywhere else, so there is nothing further to delete on our side. You can also clear the app's storage in your phone's settings or uninstall the app. The same instructions are at the deletion page linked below.

"Sign out" on its own keeps your tasks and notes on the phone, so signing in again brings them back.

Your subscription and free trial belong to your Google account and are managed in Google Play (Play Store > Payments & subscriptions), so they are not affected by deleting the app's data and come back when you sign in again. Deleting data does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in Google Play. If you want your Google account to stop sharing information with the app, remove Aurelia under "Sign in with Google" in your Google Account's security settings.

10. Security

Your data lives inside the app's private storage on your phone, protected by Android. Sign-in is handled by Google. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so please also keep your phone locked and up to date.

11. Children

Aurelia is not meant for children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from them or direct advertising at them. If you believe a child has signed in, please contact us.

12. Your choices and rights

Because your content is stored on your own phone, you control it: you can view, change and delete it in the app at any time, and you can erase everything with "Delete my data and sign out" in Profile. You may withdraw your consent by deleting your data and uninstalling the app.

Depending on where you live (for example the United Kingdom, or a US state with a privacy law such as California), you may have the right to know what we hold about you, to correct or delete it, to withdraw consent, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to your local data-protection authority. The only personal information we can see about you is your name and email address from Google sign-in, and they are kept only on your phone, so you can exercise these rights yourself in the app. If you would like us to confirm anything or to help, contact us at the address below and we will reply within 30 days. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

To cancel your subscription or free trial, use Google Play (Play Store > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions).

13. Using the app in the United Kingdom, the United States and other countries

We do not have a server that receives your data, so your tasks, notes, drawings and chat history never leave your phone and are not moved between countries by us. Google sign-in, Google Play, the speech-recognition service and the advertising service are run by their providers, who may process information in other countries under their own policies and safeguards.

Our reasons for handling your name and email are: your consent when you sign in, and what is needed to give you the app and check your subscription. We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it for advertising, other than what the advertising service on the Home screen collects from your phone itself (see section 8, where you can also limit it).

The app is for adults. We do not knowingly offer it to anyone under 18.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when we add a feature. The "Effective" date above will change, and for important changes we will tell you in the app. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
